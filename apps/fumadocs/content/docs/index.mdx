RevKlik is privacy-first web and revenue analytics: no third-party cookies, no
cross-site tracking, no ad profiles. Point the tracking snippet at your site and
the dashboard answers the two questions that matter: **who is visiting**, and
**how do visits turn into revenue**.

## What you get

- **Web analytics**: visitors, pageviews, bounce rate, and visit duration with
  automatic comparison against the previous period, broken down by page, source,
  geography, and platform.
- **Revenue attribution**: conversions carry revenue from payment gateways
  (Midtrans, Xendit, DOKU, Mayar, Paywuz, Polar, Stripe, Lemon Squeezy) and
  App Store Connect imports into the same revenue panels. Attribution and
  funnels start on the Starter plan.
- **Visitor journeys**: step-by-step timelines with tags and notes your team
  can attach.
- **Scroll funnels**: funnels derived automatically from the sections of your
  pages, so you can see where visitors stop scrolling without building a funnel
  by hand.
- **Run-rate forecast**: your recent daily revenue pace extended over the next
  30 days, in your site's currency.
- **Growth extras**: heatmaps, session recordings, and AI insights on the Growth
  plan.
