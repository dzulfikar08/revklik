## Run-rate forecast

The **Run-rate forecast** panel on the dashboard home extends your recent
sales pace over the next 30 days, in your site's currency. It is arithmetic
on real revenue, not a model:

- It averages the full days since your site started recording, up to the
  last 30. Today is left out because it is still filling.
- It needs at least 7 full days of data and 3 sales. Before that the panel
  says what is missing instead of showing a number.
- It ignores seasonality, campaigns and refunds still to come. Treat it as
  "where my pace is heading", not a promise.

It is available on every plan.

## AI insights

The **AI insights** panel summarises patterns from your events. It is a
**Growth plan** feature; other plans see a blurred preview with an _Upgrade to
Growth_ button. Insights are written in the language you have selected
(English or Bahasa Indonesia) and refresh on demand via **Refresh**. Each
insight card carries a type, a confidence percentage, a summary, and an
optional action line:

| Type                | What it looks for                |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Funnel optimization | Where the funnel leaks.          |
| Geo opportunities   | Regions worth investing in.      |
| Revenue drivers     | What actually generates revenue. |
| Traffic ROI         | Which traffic pays off.          |

The panel header summarises the data window it studied (event count and
revenue). Insights need a minimum of data. With too few events the panel
says: _Not enough data yet. Check back after 100+ events._
