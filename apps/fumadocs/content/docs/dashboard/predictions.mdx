Two panels on the dashboard home turn your events into forward-looking
answers. Both are **Growth plan** features — other plans see a blurred
preview with an _Upgrade to Growth_ button.

## Revenue Predictions

The **Revenue Predictions** panel scores your top five visitors by
conversion likelihood:

- **Score** — predicted conversion probability as a percentage, colour-coded
  (green at 70%+, mid-tone at 40%+).
- **Predicted LTV** — the visitor's predicted lifetime value.
- **Factors** — hover a row to see what moved the score.
- **Model** — the footer names the model version and its generated time.

Click a visitor to open their [journey](/dashboard/visitors) and see how they
actually behave. **Refresh** re-runs scoring on demand.

## AI insights

The **AI insights** panel summarises patterns from your events — it refreshes
automatically every five minutes and on demand via **Refresh**. Each insight
card carries a type, a confidence percentage, a summary, and an optional
action line:

| Type                | What it looks for                |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Funnel optimization | Where the funnel leaks.          |
| Geo opportunities   | Regions worth investing in.      |
| Revenue drivers     | What actually generates revenue. |
| Revenue prediction  | Where revenue is heading.        |
| Traffic ROI         | Which traffic pays off.          |

The panel header summarises the data window it studied (event count and
revenue). Insights need a minimum of data — with too few events the panel
says: _Not enough data yet. Check back after 100+ events._
