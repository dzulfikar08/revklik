---
title: Sites
description: Add, snippet, and manage the sites you track in RevKlik, including self-traffic exclusions and purging your own visits.
---

All tracked sites live under **Manage sites** (open it from the site picker in
the dashboard's top bar).

## Add a site

Enter a **name** (e.g. `My blog`) and a **domain** (e.g. `example.com`) and
click **Add site**. A pasted URL is reduced to its domain (scheme, path, and
trailing slash removed) and lowercased. Domains must be unique; a duplicate
is rejected with _That domain is already added_. If your plan's site limit is
reached, RevKlik says so and offers an **Upgrade** button.

## Snippet

The _Tracking snippet · domain_ modal opens as soon as a site is added, and
every row has a **Snippet** button to open it again. The snippet is generated
per site; copy it and paste it into your site's HTML. While the modal is
open it waits for the site's first pageview and confirms when it arrives.
Sites that have not received any data yet are marked _Not installed_ on the
Sites page. See
[getting started](/getting-started#install-the-tracking-snippet) for the
full walkthrough.

## Row stats

Each site shows its visitor count, view count, and revenue (when there is
any) so you can compare properties at a glance.

## Exclusions and purging

Expand a row to manage your own traffic:

- **Exclude this device**: the current browser stops being tracked from now
  on. The device is matched by a keyed hash of IP, user agent, and language;
  excluded devices are listed with per-item remove.
- **Purge my past visits**: deletes all events, sessions, and visitor
  profiles already recorded for this device.

## Delete a site

The trash icon deletes the site **and all of its data** after an inline
confirmation. The tracking snippet stops reporting from that property.
