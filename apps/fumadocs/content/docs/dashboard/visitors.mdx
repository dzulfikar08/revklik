title: Visitors and journeys
description: Explore individual klikapa visitors — pseudonymous identity, step-by-step journeys, tags, notes, and conversions.

The **Explore** panel on the dashboard has two tabs: **Users** and
**Journeys**. Both respect the active range, filters, and segments.

## Users

The Users table lists recent visitors with:

- **Visitor** — avatar and display name with an event-count chip.
- **Source** — where the visit came from.
- **Spent** — total attributed revenue.
- **Last seen** — relative time plus seven days of activity dots.

## Identity

Visitors without a known name get a **deterministic pseudonym** — the same
visitor always resolves to the same colour-animal name (like *cobalt
quokka*) and a matching avatar, generated from their visitor ID. Visitors
with revenue get a **Customer** badge; identified visitors show their email,
everyone else shows *not identified*.

## Journeys

The Journeys tab shows the latest sessions as readable rows:

- a country flag and a **source chip** (UTM source, referrer, or *direct*),
- up to six **step chips** joined with arrows,
- an event count, and
- a green revenue badge when the session converted, or *Exited*.

## The visitor profile

Click any user or journey to open the full profile, *Journey · name*:

- **Identity card** — avatar, name, Customer badge, email, country, city,
  device, OS, and browser.
- **Parameters** — custom event parameters, with sensitive values masked.
- **Tags & Notes** — add lowercased tags and a free-text note for your team;
  everything saves per visitor.
- **Timeline** — every event in order (*Viewed page*, *Scrolled to 75%*,
  *Read section pricing*, *Clicked Sign up*, *Conversion: checkout* with its
  revenue, *Triggered event: ...*). Flip the order with
  *Oldest/Newest first*, or **Copy** to grab the journey as JSON.

## Sorting

Use the sort toggle to order by **Recent** or by **Events** — handy for
finding your most engaged visitors in a busy range.
