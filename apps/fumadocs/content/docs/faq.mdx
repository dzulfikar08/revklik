## Is RevKlik privacy-friendly?

RevKlik is built to collect as little as possible: no third-party cookies, no
cross-site tracking, no ad profiles. Each browser gets a random first-party
visitor ID, the dashboard shows visitors under pseudonyms (names and avatars,
not raw identifiers), custom event parameters with sensitive values are masked,
and you can exclude your own devices and purge your past visits from
[Sites](/dashboard/sites#exclusions-and-purging). A persistent ID can still
single out a returning visitor, so it counts as personal data. The
[privacy page](/privacy) lists what is stored and for how long.

## Does RevKlik use cookies?

The tracker does not set third-party cookies. It keeps a random visitor ID in
the browser's local storage on your own domain, plus a session ID in session
storage. Both are first-party and never shared between sites.

## Do I need a consent banner?

It depends on where your visitors live. If your audience includes Indonesian,
EU or UK residents, we recommend asking for consent before the tracker runs:
add `data-consent="required"` to the script tag and call
`klikapa.consent(true)` when the visitor accepts. Until then nothing is stored
and nothing is sent. This is guidance, not legal advice; see
[privacy and consent](/privacy#consent) for the exact snippet.

## Why does my dashboard show no data?

The usual causes are an ad blocker, a Content Security Policy that blocks
`app.revklik.com`, a wrong site id, a consent gate that never opens, or testing
on `localhost`. [Getting started](/getting-started#no-data-check) has a checklist.

## What events does the tracking script collect?

Pageviews, clicks, scroll depth, which page sections a visitor reads, custom
events you trigger, and conversions (which can carry revenue). Internal
heartbeat events are tracked for session health but hidden from the UI.

## How do funnels work, and do I have to build one?

No. Funnels are derived automatically from the `data-section` markers on your
pages. Add the attributes, and the scroll funnel appears. See
[funnels](/dashboard/funnels). Funnels and revenue attribution start on the
Starter plan.

## Where does revenue data come from?

Four places: conversions with a revenue amount, payment-gateway webhooks
([Xendit, DOKU, Mayar, Paywuz, Polar](/integrations#payments)),
the [server events API](/integrations#server-events-api) for App Store and
backend purchases, and [App Store Connect](/integrations#apps) imports. You can
also [connect Polar](/integrations#polar) to book its orders, fees and refunds.

## Does RevKlik forecast my revenue?

Yes, in a simple way. The [run-rate forecast](/dashboard/insights) averages your
recent full days of revenue and extends that pace over the next 30 days, in
your site's currency. It is arithmetic on your real sales, not a model, and it
needs at least 7 full days of data and 3 sales.

## Can I share my dashboard?

Yes: a read-only **public dashboard link**, an embeddable **live widget**,
and scoped **API tokens**. See [share and access](/integrations#share-and-access).

## Can I query RevKlik from an AI coding agent?

Yes. RevKlik has an [MCP server](/mcp) at
`https://app.revklik.com/api/mcp` with 14 read-only tools mirroring the REST
API. Connect Codex, Cursor, or Claude with an API token from the Share tab.

## Can I get my data out?

The dashboard's export button downloads raw events for the current range and
filters as CSV. API tokens with the `analytics:export` scope allow
programmatic exports. You can also delete a visitor, a site, or your account;
see [privacy](/privacy#deletion-and-backups).

## What's the difference between Micro, Starter, and Growth?

Limits and features. All three are paid: Micro (1 site, 10K events/month,
Rp 16.000 or $1), Starter (3 sites, 100K events/month, Rp 64.000 or $4), and
Growth (10 sites, 500K events/month, Rp 192.000 or $12), each per month.
**Growth** additionally unlocks heatmaps, session recordings, and AI insights.
Details in [billing](/billing).

## Is there a free plan or a trial?

There is no free plan. New accounts get a **7-day free trial of Growth**, no
card required. When a trial or subscription ends, RevKlik keeps recording
visits for 14 days while the dashboard stays locked. Once you pick a plan,
you see data within that plan's retention window (Micro 7 days, Starter 30,
Growth 90), so a longer gap on a shorter plan means older days are not
shown. See
[after a trial or subscription ends](/billing#after-a-trial-or-subscription-ends).

## How do I pay in Indonesia?

With **QRIS** (RevKlik opens a QRIS payment page and verifies your payment).
QRIS prices are in IDR (Rp 16.000 Micro / Rp 64.000 Starter / Rp 192.000 Growth
per month), monthly, cancel anytime. Card checkout in USD ($1 / $4 / $12 per
month) is the option for everyone else.

## Can I import from another analytics tool?

Yes. Historical data can be imported from **OneDollarStats** (visitors,
pages, countries, sources), and **Polar** orders can be synced once the site
is [connected](/integrations#polar). See [imports](/integrations#imports).

## How does RevKlik know which Google searches perform best?

Connect **Google Search Console** under Integrations → Search and sync to pull
clicks, impressions, CTR, and position per query.

## Can my team use the same account?

Invite teammates from **Settings → Team** by email as Admin, Editor, or
Viewer. Invitees need an existing RevKlik account.

## How do I cancel?

Billing view → **Cancel subscription** → confirm. The plan stays active until
the end of the period and simply doesn't renew. See
[managing your subscription](/billing#managing-your-subscription).
