RevKlik is privacy-first analytics: no third-party cookies, no cross-site
tracking, no ad profiles. This page says plainly what the tracker stores, so you
can describe it correctly in your own privacy notice.

## The visitor ID

RevKlik gives each browser a random ID, kept in the browser's local storage on
your own domain (first-party, not a cookie). It lets you count returning
visitors and tie a sale to the visit that caused it, even days later. The ID is
random and is never shared between sites.

If the visitor clears browser storage, our server can still recognise them with
a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256 with a secret salt that is different for every site)
of IP address, browser, language and screen size. The raw IP address is not
stored, and the hash cannot be matched across sites.

A persistent ID can single out a returning visitor, so it counts as personal
data under Indonesia's PDP Law (UU 27/2022) and GDPR. RevKlik keeps that data
minimal; it does not make it disappear.

## What is stored and for how long

We store pageviews, custom events, sessions and visitor profiles for your site,
plus country, region and city from Cloudflare, device, browser and OS. Names,
emails and phone numbers are stored only if you send them through
`klikapa.identify()`.

Raw events are deleted automatically after a retention period that depends on
your plan. It is twice the reporting window of the plan, with a 30-day minimum:

| Plan    | Dashboard window | Raw events kept |
| ------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| Micro   | 7 days           | 30 days         |
| Starter | 30 days          | 60 days         |
| Growth  | 90 days          | 180 days        |

Payment and revenue records stay as bookkeeping records until you delete the
site or the account.

## Deletion and backups

You can delete a single visitor's data, a whole site, or your account from
Settings. Live data is removed immediately. Payment records tied to a deleted
visitor are kept but anonymised.

Database backups are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and the last 30 daily backups are
kept, so deleted data disappears from backups within 30 days.

## Where data lives

Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure (D1 database, Asia-Pacific primary
region), so it is processed outside Indonesia. Say so in your privacy notice.

## Consent: guidance for site owners [#consent]

<Callout type="warn" title="Not legal advice">
	This is guidance for site owners, not legal advice. Ask a lawyer who knows the rules where your
	visitors live.
</Callout>

You are the data controller for your visitors' data; RevKlik is your processor.
Under Indonesia's PDP Law (UU 27/2022) you need a lawful basis (consent is the
safest) and a privacy notice that names the tools you use. What we recommend:

- Mention RevKlik, the first-party visitor ID and the retention periods above in
  your privacy policy.
- If your audience includes Indonesian, EU or UK residents, ask for consent
  before the tracker runs. Add `data-consent="required"` to the script tag and
  call `klikapa.consent(true)` when the visitor accepts. Until then nothing is
  stored and nothing is sent. If the visitor declines, call
  `klikapa.consent(false)`.
- Always get consent before calling `identify()` (name, email, phone) and before
  turning on heatmaps or session recordings. Both are off until you set
  `window.klikapa_consent`.
- The tracker honours Global Privacy Control (`Sec-GPC`) and Do Not Track by
  default.

Here is the consent setup in full. Load the tracker in consent mode:

```html
<script
	defer
	data-site-id="YOUR_SITE_ID"
	data-consent="required"
	src="https://app.revklik.com/klikapa.js"
></script>
```

Then call it from your consent banner:

```js
// the visitor accepts
klikapa.consent(true);

// the visitor declines, or withdraws consent later
klikapa.consent(false);
```

`klikapa.consent(true)` remembers the choice in the browser, so later page loads
resume without calling it again. `klikapa.consent(false)` clears the visitor ID
and stops sending. To allow heatmaps and session recordings once the visitor has
agreed to them:

```js
window.klikapa_consent = { heatmap: true, recording: true };
```
