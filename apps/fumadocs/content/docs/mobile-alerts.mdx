title: Mobile sale alerts
description: "Get a push notification on your phone for every sale and refund across all your sites, with per-account toggles and a test button."
---

The RevKlik mobile app (iOS and Android) pings your phone the moment money moves, for **every
site you own or have been invited to**, in one place. It also has an earnings view across all of
your products.

## What triggers an alert

Any revenue event that is not zero, stored from a verified source:

- a **sale** (positive revenue) shows `New sale · <site name>`
- a **refund** (negative revenue) shows `Refund · <site name>`

The message body reads `<product or event name> · <amount> · net <net>`. The amount is in the
event's own currency (IDR without decimals). The `net` part is left out when it equals the gross
amount or is unknown. Tapping the notification opens the Earnings tab.

Only verified sources alert: the authenticated [server events API](/integrations) (`POST
/api/v1/events/server`) and the signature-verified Polar, Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Midtrans, Xendit,
DOKU, Mayar and Paywuz webhooks. Revenue sent by the browser tracker (`/track`, `/track/payment`)
never alerts, because anyone who knows a site id can call those endpoints. A retried delivery of
the same payment (same `external_id` or provider order) never alerts twice.

## Who gets them

The site owner and every team member who accepted their invitation, as long as that person has
signed in on the app (which registers the phone) and has the matching toggle on. Alerts are not
gated on your plan: a lapsed account still hears about money arriving.

## Turning alerts on and off

In the app, **Settings** has two switches, **Sales** and **Refunds** (both on by default), and a
**Send test notification** button that pushes to every phone signed in to your account. Signing
out removes that phone.

## Self-hosting

Alerts go through the [Expo push service](https://docs.expo.dev/push-notifications/overview/),
which relays to Apple and Google with the credentials of the app's Expo project. Nothing to
configure for the default setup. If you enable "enhanced push security" on the Expo project, set
the `EXPO_ACCESS_TOKEN` secret on the Worker. The Worker's `*/5 * * * *` cron trigger retries
failed sends and removes phones that no longer accept pushes, so keep it in your wrangler config.

## API

All endpoints take a session token (`Authorization: Bearer kp_...`).

| Method and path            | Body                                                                          | Result                               |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `POST /api/push/devices`   | `{ "expo_token", "platform": "ios" \| "android", "locale?", "app_version?" }` | `{ "success": true }`                |
| `DELETE /api/push/devices` | `{ "expo_token" }`                                                            | `{ "success": true }`                |
| `GET /api/push/prefs`      |                                                                               | `{ "sales": true, "refunds": true }` |
| `PUT /api/push/prefs`      | `{ "sales?": boolean, "refunds?": boolean }`                                  | the saved prefs                      |
| `POST /api/push/test`      |                                                                               | `{ "sent", "tickets" }`              |

Registering a token that another account used before moves it to the current account.
