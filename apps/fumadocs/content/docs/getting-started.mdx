## 1. Create an account

Sign up at [app.revklik.com/register](https://app.revklik.com/register) with
an email address and password, or continue with Google. Every new account
starts with a **7-day free trial of Growth, no card required**. The
dashboard is available in English and Bahasa Indonesia (switch with the
ID/EN toggle).

## 2. Add your site

On the **Sites** page, click **Add site** (on a site's dashboard, open the
site picker in the top bar and choose **Manage sites**). Fill in:

- **Name**: anything that helps you recognise the site, e.g. `My blog`.
- **Domain**: e.g. `example.com`. You can paste a full URL: the scheme,
  path, and trailing slash are stripped and the domain is lowercased. Each
  domain can only be added once.

Click **Add site** and the tracking snippet opens straight away.

## 3. Install the tracking snippet

The modal titled _Tracking snippet · your-domain_ shows a snippet generated
for that site:

> Paste this into your site's HTML. Data starts with the first visit.

Copy it with **Copy snippet** and paste it into the `<head>` of every page you
want to measure: in your templates, layout, or via your tag manager. The
script is lightweight (~4 KB gzipped). The snippet is unique per site, so
always copy it from the modal rather than from another site. To open it
again later, click **Snippet** on the site's row in **Manage sites**, or
**Install snippet** on a site card marked _Not installed_.

<Callout type="tip" title="Verify it works">
	Keep the modal open and visit your site in another tab. The modal shows *Waiting for the first
	pageview…* and switches to *First pageview received* once the first event arrives.
</Callout>

## 4. Exclude your own devices

Inside **Manage sites**, expand a site to find its exclusions:

- **Exclude this device**: stops tracking on the browser you develop from
  (the fingerprint combines IP, user-agent, and language). Run this once on
  every device you work from.
- **Purge my past visits**: deletes events, sessions, and visitor profiles
  already recorded for this device.
