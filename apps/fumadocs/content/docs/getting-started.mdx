## 1. Create an account

Sign up at [app.revklik.com](https://app.revklik.com) with an email address and
password, or continue with Google. Every new account starts with a
**free 7-day trial — no card required**.

## 2. Add your site

From the dashboard, open the site picker in the top bar and choose
**Manage sites**. Fill in:

- **Name** — anything that helps you recognise the site, e.g. `My blog`.
- **Domain** — e.g. `example.com`. Domains are lowercased, and each domain can
  only be added once.

Click **Add site** and the site appears in your list with its own per-row
visitor and view counts.

## 3. Install the tracking snippet

Click **Snippet** on your site's row. A modal titled
_Tracking snippet · your-domain_ shows a snippet generated for that site:

> Paste this into your site's HTML. Stats start flowing on the first visit.

Copy it with **Copy snippet** and paste it into the `<head>` of every page you
want to measure — in your templates, layout, or via your tag manager. The
snippet is unique per site, so always copy it from the modal rather than from
another site.

<Callout type="tip" title="Verify it works">
	Open your site in another tab and click around. Within seconds the dashboard should show visitors
	online in the top bar and new rows in the Live panel.
</Callout>

## 4. Exclude your own devices

Inside **Manage sites**, expand a site to find its exclusions:

- **Exclude this device** — stops tracking on the browser you develop from
  (the fingerprint combines IP, user-agent, and language). Run this once on
  every device you work from.
- **Purge my past visits** — deletes events, sessions, and visitor profiles
  already recorded for this device.
