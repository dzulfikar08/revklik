RevKlik is **privacy-first web and revenue analytics**: no third-party cookies,
no cross-site tracking, no ad profiles. Point the tracking snippet at your site
and the dashboard answers the two questions that matter: who is visiting, and
how do visits turn into revenue.

## What you get

- **Web analytics**: visitors, pageviews, bounce rate, and visit duration with
  automatic comparison against the previous period, broken down by page,
  source, geography, and platform.
- **Revenue analytics**: conversions carry a revenue amount, and payment
  gateways (Xendit, DOKU, Mayar, Paywuz, Polar) plus App Store Connect imports feed the same revenue panels.
- **Visitor journeys**: every visitor gets a readable identity, a step-by-step
  journey timeline, and tags/notes your team can attach.
- **Scroll funnels**: funnels are derived automatically from the sections of
  your pages, so you can see where visitors stop scrolling without building a
  funnel by hand.
- **Run-rate forecast**: your recent daily revenue pace extended over the next
  30 days, in your site's currency.
- **Growth extras**: heatmaps, session recordings, and AI insights on the
  Growth plan.
- **Realtime**: a live visitor count and event feed stream straight into the
  dashboard while your site gets traffic.

## Privacy by design

RevKlik sets no third-party cookies and does no cross-site tracking. Each
browser gets a random first-party visitor ID in local storage, with a per-site
salt for the server-side fallback, and the dashboard shows visitors under
deterministic pseudonyms (a name like _cobalt quokka_ and an avatar) instead of
raw identifiers. Form parameters are masked, raw events are deleted after 30, 60
or 180 days depending on your plan, and you can exclude your own devices and
purge your past visits. A persistent ID can still single out a returning
visitor, so treat it as personal data: the [privacy page](/privacy) explains
what is stored and how to ask for consent.
