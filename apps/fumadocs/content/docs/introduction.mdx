klikapa is **privacy-first web and revenue analytics**. Point the tracking
snippet at your site and the dashboard answers the two questions that matter:
who is visiting, and how do visits turn into revenue.

## What you get

- **Web analytics** — visitors, pageviews, bounce rate, and visit duration with
  automatic comparison against the previous period, broken down by page,
  source, geography, and platform.
- **Revenue analytics** — conversions carry a revenue amount, and payment
  providers (Midtrans, Xendit, Stripe, LemonSqueezy), marketplaces
  (Shopee, Tokopedia), and Polar orders feed the same revenue panels.
- **Visitor journeys** — every visitor gets a readable identity, a step-by-step
  journey timeline, and tags/notes your team can attach.
- **Scroll funnels** — funnels are derived automatically from the sections of
  your pages, so you can see where visitors stop scrolling without building a
  funnel by hand.
- **Growth extras** — heatmaps, session recordings, revenue predictions, and AI
  insights on the Growth plan.
- **Realtime** — a live visitor count and event feed stream straight into the
  dashboard while your site gets traffic.

## Privacy by design

Visitors are shown under deterministic pseudonyms (a name like *cobalt
quokka* and an avatar) instead of raw identifiers, form parameters are masked,
and you can exclude your own devices from tracking and purge your past visits
with one action.
