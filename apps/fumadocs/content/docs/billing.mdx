RevKlik has three paid plans: **Micro**, **Starter**, and **Growth**. There
is no free plan. Every new account gets a **7-day free trial of Growth, no
card required**, so you can try every feature before choosing a plan.

<Callout type="info" title="Self-hosting">
	Plans apply to the hosted service at app.revklik.com. A self-hosted install (`SELF_HOSTED=1`) has
	no plans, limits, trials or checkout: every feature is unlocked.
</Callout>

## Plans

| Plan    | Limits                    | API + widget tokens | QRIS (IDR)      | Card (USD) |
| ------- | ------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------- | ---------- |
| Micro   | 1 site · 10K events/mo    | 2                   | Rp 16.000 / mo  | $1 / mo    |
| Starter | 3 sites · 100K events/mo  | 5                   | Rp 64.000 / mo  | $4 / mo    |
| Growth  | 10 sites · 500K events/mo | unlimited           | Rp 192.000 / mo | $12 / mo   |

Visitors from Indonesia see IDR prices first and can pay with QRIS; everyone
else sees USD prices and pays by card. Usage bars in the billing view show
**Events this month** and **Sites** against your plan's limits.

<Callout type="tip" title="What Growth unlocks">
	Heatmaps, session recordings, and AI insights are Growth features; other plans see a blurred
	preview with an *Upgrade to Growth* shortcut. The trial includes all of them.
</Callout>

What each plan includes:

| Plan    | Dashboard window | Raw events kept | Revenue attribution and funnels | Heatmaps, recordings, AI insights |
| ------- | ---------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Micro   | 7 days           | 30 days         | No                              | No                                |
| Starter | 30 days          | 60 days         | Yes                             | No                                |
| Growth  | 90 days          | 180 days        | Yes                             | Yes                               |

The run-rate forecast is on every plan. Raw events are deleted automatically
after the retention period; see [privacy](/privacy#what-is-stored-and-for-how-long).

## Trial

- The trial starts when you create your account and runs for 7 days on
  Growth.
- The billing view shows how many days are left. You can buy any plan during
  the trial, Growth included.
- When the trial ends without a plan, the dashboard locks and shows the plans
  instead.

## After a trial or subscription ends

Tracking does not stop straight away. For **14 days** after the trial or
your subscription ends, RevKlik keeps recording visits from your sites, but
the dashboard stays locked behind the plan picker. Billing stays reachable.
When you pick a plan, the dashboard shows data within that plan's retention
window: the last 7 days on Micro, 30 days on Starter, and 90 days on Growth.
Older data is not shown, even if it was recorded during the grace period.
After the 14 days, tracking pauses until you pick a plan.

## Paying

Every plan card offers:

- **Pay with QRIS** (Indonesia only): opens the QRIS payment page in a new
  tab. Complete the payment there, then press **I've paid, check now** back
  in RevKlik. The reminder stays on the billing view, even after a reload,
  until the payment is confirmed, and it lets you reopen the payment page.
- **Pay with card**: a hosted checkout in USD, processed by Polar. After
  payment you return to RevKlik and the plan activates as soon as the payment
  is confirmed.

All plans are **monthly and cancel anytime**.

## Managing your subscription

The billing view shows `Renews on <date>` while a plan is active, or
`Access until <date>` after cancellation. **Cancel subscription** asks for a
confirmation and keeps the plan active until the end of the period: nothing
is deleted at that point, you simply stop renewing.

## If you take payments yourself

Paying for RevKlik and tracking your own sales are separate things. Your
customers' payments flow **into** RevKlik, not out of it: connect a payment
gateway under [Integrations → Payments](/integrations#payments) (Midtrans,
Xendit, DOKU, Mayar, Paywuz, Polar, Stripe, or Lemon Squeezy) and its
transactions feed your revenue panels automatically.
