RevKlik has three plans: **Micro**, **Starter**, and **Growth**. Every new
account starts on Micro with a **free 7-day trial, no card required**.

## Plans

| Plan         | Limits                    | API + widget tokens | Price                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Micro (free) | 1 site · 10K events/mo    | 2                   | free                     |
| Starter      | 3 sites · 100K events/mo  | 5                   | Rp 64.000 via QRIS / mo  |
| Growth       | 10 sites · 500K events/mo | unlimited           | Rp 192.000 via QRIS / mo |

Usage bars in the billing view show **Events this month** and **Sites**
against your plan's limits. QRIS prices are fixed; card prices are shown at
checkout.

<Callout type="tip" title="What Growth unlocks">
	Heatmaps, session recordings, revenue predictions, and AI insights are Growth features; other
	plans see a blurred preview with an *Upgrade to Growth* shortcut.
</Callout>

## Paying

Every plan card offers two buttons:

- **Pay with card**: a hosted checkout; after payment the subscription
  activates immediately.
- **Pay with QRIS**: opens the QRIS payment page in a new tab. Complete the
  payment there, then press **I've paid** to verify back in RevKlik. The
  banner lets you reopen the tab if you closed it.

All plans are **monthly and cancel anytime**.

## Managing your subscription

The subscription banner shows `Renews on <date>` while active, or
`Access until <date>` after cancellation. **Cancel subscription** asks for a
confirmation and keeps the plan active until the end of the period: nothing
is deleted at that point, you simply stop renewing.

## If you take payments yourself

The QRIS/GoPay flows **into** RevKlik, not out of it: connect a payment
provider under [Integrations → Payments](/integrations#payments) (Midtrans,
Xendit, Stripe, Lemon Squeezy) and their transactions (GoPay, QRIS, cards,
and more) feed your revenue panels automatically.
