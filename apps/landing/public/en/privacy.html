Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority

We are committed to protecting your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control your information.

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Summary

In case of any conflict between this English version and the Indonesian version, the Indonesian version prevails.

RevKlik is a revenue-first analytics platform designed for Indonesian SaaS and digital products. We built the service with privacy principles from the start: we collect the minimum data needed to provide meaningful insights, and we never sell your data to third parties.

This policy applies to all RevKlik services, including the website, dashboard, mobile app, and tracking script. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

By default, RevKlik only collects data needed to provide the analytics service. We do not collect personal data automatically. The data we process is:

  • Pageview: page URL, referrer, and visit timestamp, to understand your site traffic.
  • Event: visitor interactions that you define yourself (clicks, scrolls, conversions) through our tracking API.
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, screen size, and language, for compatibility analysis.
  • Geographic data: country and city based on IP address, which we anonymize and do not store in raw form.
  • Attribution data: UTM parameters, traffic source, and campaign data, to help you understand where visitors come from.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal data from your site visitors unless you explicitly send them through custom events.

Cookies

RevKlik offers a cookieless tracking option that you can enable in your site settings. In cookieless mode, we use a hash-based fingerprinting technique that does not store a permanent identifier in the visitor's browser.

If you choose the mode with cookies, we only use technical first-party cookies, not cross-site tracking cookies. These cookies are used to:

  • Identify visitor sessions anonymously
  • Link pageviews within one session to build the user journey
  • Store the visitor's consent preferences

We do not use third-party cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Our cookieless attribution reaches 85–90% accuracy compared to traditional cookie-based methods.

Third-Party Data

To provide a reliable and fast service, we use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: we use global edge infrastructure for hosting and delivering the tracking script. Cloudflare processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy, and we ensure that your data remains protected on their network.
  • Polar: if you connect a payment webhook, transaction data is processed by your payment provider. We only receive and store the metadata needed for revenue attribution (transaction ID, amount, and timestamp). We do not store credit card details or sensitive payment information.

We do not share your data with any other third parties for marketing or sales purposes. All sub-processors are required to comply with data processing agreements equivalent to our privacy standards.

Data Storage

Your analytics data is stored on global edge infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest. We keep your data for as long as your account is active and subscribed.

If your account is deactivated or deleted, we will:

  • Delete all analytics data within 30 days
  • Stop all tracking scripts associated with your site
  • Request data removal from the edge cache across all global locations

You can request data deletion at any time through the dashboard or by contacting our team. We process deletion requests within a maximum of 14 business days.

Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and Indonesia's UU PDP (Personal Data Protection Law), you have the following rights over your data:

  • Right of access: you can request a copy of all data we store about you or your site at any time.
  • Right to export: export your analytics data in CSV or JSON format directly from the dashboard, at any time without limits.
  • Right to deletion: you have the right to request deletion of all your data from our systems, including analytics data and account information.
  • Right to correction: if the data we store is inaccurate, you can request a correction or update.
  • Right to portability: you can move your data to another platform in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: you can object to certain data processing that is not necessary to provide the core service.

To exercise the rights above, please contact us at support@revklik.app or visit our Support page.

Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or applicable legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Send a notification by email to the address registered on your account
  • Show a notice in the RevKlik dashboard when you log in
  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page

Your continued use of our services after a policy change means you accept the updated version. We recommend that you review this policy periodically.
