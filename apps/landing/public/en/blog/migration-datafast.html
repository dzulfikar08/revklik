Migrating from DataFast to RevKlik: Cheaper and More Complete

May 14, 2026 7 min read By the RevKlik Team

DataFast is an analytics tool focused on speed. But speed alone is not enough when you need to understand how visitors interact with your site and how much revenue each channel generates.

RevKlik offers everything DataFast has, plus revenue attribution and user journey, at a much lower price.

Why Move Away from DataFast?

DataFast is fast. But it lacks a few critical features:

  • Cookieless accuracy: DataFast still uses some traditional tracking techniques. RevKlik was built 100% cookieless from the start.
  • Price: DataFast charges $9/month for 50K events. RevKlik is only Rp 16.000/month for the same limit, with more features.
If you pay $9/month for analytics without revenue attribution, you are paying too much for too little insight.

3 Migration Steps

Step 1

Get the RevKlik Tracking Script

Create a RevKlik account. In the dashboard, you immediately get a unique tracking script for your site:

<script defer data-site-id="YOUR-SITE-ID"
  src="https://app.revklik.com/klikapa.js"></script>

Copy this script. You will need it in the next step.

Step 2

Swap the DataFast Script for RevKlik

Open your site's code and find the DataFast script:

Before (DataFast)
<script defer data-website-id="YOUR-DATAFAST-ID"
  src="https://datafast.app/insights.js"></script>

Replace it with the RevKlik script:

After (RevKlik)
<script defer data-site-id="YOUR-SITE-ID"
  src="https://app.revklik.com/klikapa.js"></script>

Deploy the change. Data starts flowing into the RevKlik dashboard within seconds.

Step 3

Import Your Goals/Events Configuration

If you have custom events or goals in DataFast, recreate them in the RevKlik dashboard. The process is simple:

  • Open Settings → Events in the RevKlik dashboard
  • Add the same event names you have in DataFast
  • Events are recorded automatically once visitors trigger the action

For revenue attribution, connect the Polar webhook in the Integrations menu. Done.

FAQ

How long does the migration take?

Less than 5 minutes. Swap the script (1 minute) + import events (2 minutes) + deploy (1 minute). No server configuration or database setup.

Will I lose my data?

No data is lost during the migration. Your old data in DataFast stays accessible in their dashboard. RevKlik starts recording new data from the moment the script is installed.

What about technical support?

All users get email support. Growth plan users get priority support with a response time under 2 hours on business days.

Can I run DataFast and RevKlik at the same time?

Yes. Add the RevKlik script alongside DataFast during the transition. Both scripts are light and will not affect your site's performance. Once the RevKlik data is verified (2-3 days), remove the DataFast script.

Why is RevKlik cheaper?

RevKlik runs on a global edge network, not on centralized servers. This means much lower infrastructure costs, and we pass the savings on to users. Edge also means lower latency for your visitors, wherever they are.
