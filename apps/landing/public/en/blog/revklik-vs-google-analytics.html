RevKlik vs Google Analytics 4: Which Fits Indonesian SaaS?

May 14, 2026 9 min read By the RevKlik Team

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) holds more than 80% of the web analytics market. It's free, familiar, and "good enough" for most people. But if you're building a SaaS or digital product that needs to know which channel makes money, not just how many pageviews you got, GA4 has a big gap.

This isn't just a feature list. We compare the two platforms from the angle that matters most to founders and makers: site performance, revenue attribution, privacy, and day-to-day use.

1. Performance: Script Size and Its Impact on Your Site

The GA4 script (gtag.js) is about 45 KB after gzip. That's not small. Every time a visitor opens your page, the browser has to download, parse, and execute that script before the page is truly ready. On a 3G connection or Indonesia's uneven networks, this can add 50-200ms to your Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).

RevKlik is about 4 KB (gzip). Our script loads deferred and non-blocking, and is served from a global edge network with nodes in Jakarta, Singapore, and 300+ other locations. Impact on LCP? Barely noticeable, under 5ms.

Why this matters

Google itself says that every 100ms of delay lowers the conversion rate by 1%. If your analytics slows your site down, you're losing money to track money.

2. Revenue Attribution: Where Does Your Money Come From?

This is the biggest difference between the two platforms.

GA4 can track purchase events, but real revenue attribution requires:

  • Export to BigQuery ($0.01/GB after the free tier)
  • Special setup in GA4 Admin > Attribution Settings
  • SQL or Looker Studio to visualize it
  • An understanding of the data-driven attribution model

This process can take days to set up, and it needs technical skills that not every founder has.

RevKlik is built with revenue as a first-class citizen. Connect the Polar webhook, and every payment is automatically linked to the visitor's user journey. You immediately know:

  • Which UTM source/campaign generates revenue
  • Which pages are visited most often before conversion
  • The funnel from first visit to payment
  • Revenue per channel, per campaign, per landing page

No BigQuery. No SQL. It's all in the dashboard from day one.

3. Privacy and Compliance

GA4 needs cookies to track user sessions. In Indonesia, the PDP Law (UU PDP) requires you to get consent before storing cookies. That means you need to:

  1. Install a cookie consent banner
  2. Enable GA4 Consent Mode
  3. Make sure the banner and analytics are integrated correctly
  4. Keep proof of consent for audits

RevKlik uses no cookies at all. Tracking is based on server-side fingerprinting that complies with GDPR and the PDP Law by default. No banner needed. No consent mode. Your visitors aren't interrupted by the "Accept Cookies?" popup that everyone hates.

Accuracy? RevKlik's cookieless attribution reaches 85-90%, more than enough to make business decisions.

4. Day-to-Day Use: The Dashboard

GA4 is a powerful tool, but it is known for being hard to use. A simple analysis like "how much revenue did campaign X bring in this month?" takes a custom exploration, filters, and several clicks. Its interface is designed for analysts, not for founders who want a quick answer.

RevKlik is designed on a different principle: open the dashboard, see the answer. The dashboard shows:

  • Real-time visitors and active pages
  • Top revenue channels and campaigns
  • A visual conversion funnel
  • Scroll depth per page section
  • Daily/weekly/monthly revenue trends

Time from "open the dashboard" to "I know which channel to focus on": under 30 seconds.

5. When to Choose GA4

We'll be honest: GA4 isn't a bad choice for everyone. GA4 fits better if:

  • Your analytics budget is truly zero and your events are under 10 million/month
  • Your team has a data analyst who is comfortable with BigQuery and Looker Studio
  • You need deep integration with the Google ecosystem (Ads, Search Console, Optimize)
  • Your site is a content site that doesn't need revenue tracking

6. When to Choose RevKlik

RevKlik fits better if:

  • You sell something (SaaS, digital product, subscription) and need to know which channel makes money
  • Site performance is a priority: you don't want analytics slowing down loading
  • You want privacy compliance without the hassle
  • Your team is small and has no time to learn GA4 in depth
  • You want to see funnels and conversions visually, not as numbers in a table
  • Many of your visitors are from Indonesia and Southeast Asia (edge in Jakarta!)

Conclusion

GA4 is general-purpose analytics that is powerful but complex. For large teams with a dedicated analyst, it's a solid choice. But for Indonesian founders and makers building SaaS or digital products, RevKlik gives you what GA4 doesn't easily give: direct answers about money.

How much revenue came from the Twitter campaign this week? Which funnel has the most drop-off? Which pages are visited most often before payment? These questions should be answered in seconds, not hours.

FAQ: Common Questions

Can RevKlik be used alongside GA4?

Of course. Many users run both platforms in parallel during the transition. The RevKlik script (about 4 KB gzip) won't interfere with the performance of your existing GA4. You can compare data from both platforms and migrate gradually.

How accurate is RevKlik's cookieless tracking?

RevKlik's cookieless attribution reaches 85-90% accuracy. It uses a combination of server-side fingerprinting and probabilistic matching. For most business decisions, this accuracy is more than enough. Compare that with cookie-based tracking, which is becoming less accurate because of ad blockers and ITP (Intelligent Tracking Prevention) in Safari and Firefox.

Does RevKlik work for Indonesian websites?

Yes. RevKlik has an edge node in Jakarta, which means the script loads in milliseconds for visitors from Indonesia. The dashboard also shows geographic data for each city in Indonesia, something GA4 doesn't show by default.

How do I migrate from GA4 to RevKlik?

Migration is simple: add one line of the RevKlik script to your site and data starts flowing within seconds. No need to export or import data. For historical GA4 data, you can keep it in BigQuery as an archive. Read the full guide in our article on migrating from other analytics.

Why does RevKlik use Polar for revenue tracking?

Polar provides webhooks that can be sent in real time whenever a payment happens. This lets RevKlik link each transaction to the user journey directly, without a complex payment gateway integration. If you use another payment processor, you can send conversion events manually via the API.
