Migrating from Plausible to RevKlik: 3 Steps

May 14, 2026 6 min read By the RevKlik Team

Plausible is a good analytics tool: light, cookie-free, and respectful of privacy. But as your business grows, you need more than pageviews and bounce rate. You need to know how much revenue each page generates, how users move through the funnel, and where they drop out.

RevKlik was built to answer those questions, and migrating takes just 3 simple steps.

Why Move Away from Plausible?

Plausible focuses on simplicity. That works well for a personal blog, but for a growing SaaS or digital product, several important things are missing:

  • Revenue attribution: Plausible cannot connect traffic to payments. You do not know whether visitors from Google Ads actually paid.
  • User journey: there is no visualization of a visitor's path from the first page to conversion.
  • Scroll-depth funnel: you cannot tell how far visitors read on your landing page.
Plausible answers "how many visitors?" RevKlik answers "how much revenue?" That difference changes business decisions.

3 Migration Steps

Step 1

Export Your Plausible Data (Optional)

If you want to keep your historical data, open your Plausible dashboard → Settings → Export Data. Download the CSV file that contains all your visit statistics.

This step is optional because you will not lose any data during the migration. Plausible keeps running as long as you have not removed its script.

Step 2

Swap the Plausible Script for RevKlik

Open your site's HTML. Find the Plausible script tag, which looks like this:

Before (Plausible)
<script defer data-domain="domainanda.com"
  src="https://plausible.io/js/script.js"></script>

Replace it with the RevKlik script:

After (RevKlik)
<script defer data-site-id="YOUR-SITE-ID"
  src="https://app.revklik.com/klikapa.js"></script>

That is all. One line removed, one line added. Deploy the change and RevKlik starts collecting data within seconds.

Step 3

Connect the Polar Webhook

This is the step that sets RevKlik apart from Plausible. In the RevKlik dashboard, open the Integrations page and add a webhook endpoint for your payment processor.

Go to your Polar dashboard → Settings → Webhooks → Add endpoint.

Once connected, every payment is automatically attributed to the correct traffic source. Done.

Can You Run Both at the Same Time?

Yes. If you want to compare data during the transition, just add the RevKlik script alongside the Plausible script. Both are light and will not slow your site down significantly.

Both running together
<!-- Plausible (temporary) -->
<script defer data-domain="domainanda.com"
  src="https://plausible.io/js/script.js"></script>

<!-- RevKlik -->
<script defer data-site-id="YOUR-SITE-ID"
  src="https://app.revklik.com/klikapa.js"></script>

Once you are confident the RevKlik data is accurate (usually 2-3 days), remove the Plausible script.

FAQ

Will I lose my historical data?

No. Your old data in Plausible stays where it is. RevKlik starts collecting new data from the moment the script is installed. If you want to import your history, the CSV export from Plausible can be used as a reference.

Can I run Plausible and RevKlik at the same time?

Yes. The two scripts run independently and do not interfere with each other. Both are light: Plausible under 1 KB, RevKlik about 4 KB (gzip). Run both during the transition, then remove Plausible once you are comfortable.

What about GDPR compliance?

Both platforms are GDPR compliant. RevKlik is also compliant with Indonesia's PDP Law. No cookies are used, no personal data is collected, and all data is processed on the global edge network.

How long does the migration take?

Less than 5 minutes. Swap the script (1 minute) + connect the webhook (2 minutes) + deploy (1 minute). Data starts flowing right after deploy.

Can RevKlik be self-hosted like Plausible?

RevKlik runs as a managed service on a global edge network, so you do not need to manage your own servers. The benefits: lower latency, higher uptime, and we cover the infrastructure costs.
